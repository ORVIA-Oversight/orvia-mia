MIA PRESENCE

BUILT WITH YOU. GROUNDED IN YOU.

An expressly authorised digital representation created with the person themselves — never marketed as immortality or “bringing somebody back”.

Presence is not currently presented as an operational retail product. It remains subject to technical, legal, ethical, safeguarding and continuity acceptance gates.

What Presence is intended to be.

A carefully bounded interface grounded in a person’s own approved recordings, writing, memories and facts, with provenance behind every synthetic response.

What it must never claim.

  • “Live forever”
  • “Bring somebody back”
  • Digital immortality
  • “Talk to the dead”

The response model.

Recorded

The person actually said it.

Grounded

A response is assembled from approved source material.

Reflection

Only where expressly authorised and clearly disclosed.

Unknown

The system says when it does not have enough evidence.

A clear launch boundary.

Interactive deceased-parent Presence for children is not a launch product. Presence should not be sold until independent acceptance gates are passed.
