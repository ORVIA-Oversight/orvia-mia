MIA LIFE STORY

THERE ARE THINGS ONLY YOU CAN TELL.

Your story. Told by you. Professionally supported without turning a human life into a template.

Not just for the end of life.

Life Story is for healthy adults, older people, parents, grandparents, people planning ahead and people living with serious illness. The common thread is simple: there are experiences only you can describe.

  • Guided interviews
  • Remote or professional home/studio recording
  • Family history and chronology
  • Voice, video and photographs
  • Future messages
  • Preparation of a carefully sourced MIA Canon for future authorised uses
