Cancellation

How to cancel a MIA service.

This page explains the normal cancellation position for consumers buying the current MIA services online.

Normal position: a consumer service bought online will generally have a 14-day cancellation period beginning after the contract is made, subject to applicable law and exceptions.

How to cancel

Email hello@orvia.org.uk and make it clear that you want to cancel. You do not have to use the model wording below.

If you ask us to start early

We will not treat payment alone as your request for MIA to begin work during the cancellation period. If you expressly request an early start and later cancel, you may have to pay a proportionate amount for the service supplied up to cancellation. If the service has been fully performed after your express request and acknowledgement, the cancellation right may end once full performance is complete.

Refunds

Where a cancellation right applies, refunds are handled in accordance with the applicable consumer rules. Any lawful deduction or proportionate charge for service already supplied will depend on the circumstances and whether you expressly requested an early start.
