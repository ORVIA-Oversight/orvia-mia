WHY MIA EXISTS

This started with Mia.

MIA began with one dad wanting his daughter to know that years apart never meant years forgotten. It started as somewhere to preserve photographs, stories, birthday thoughts, family history and the ordinary memories a parent carries even when circumstances mean they cannot share them at the time.

Then the question became bigger: what about a parent deployed thousands of miles away, a grandparent whose stories have never been recorded, someone working offshore, a family separated by distance, or simply somebody who wants their children to hear their story in their own voice one day?

HOW MIA WORKS

Capture the human truth. Keep control of what happens next.

1

Capture

Talk. Write. Record. Photograph. Upload.

2

Preserve

Keep the original source. Never silently replace it with an edited or synthetic version.

3

Choose

You decide who may see it, when they may see it, and what technology may do with it.

4

Share

Today, next birthday, at 18, years from now, or according to the rules you deliberately set.

SOURCE BEFORE SYNTHESIS

If you didn't say it, MIA shouldn't say you did.

The human being remains the source of truth. Technology may organise approved material, but it should not manufacture memory.

RECORDED

You actually said it.

GROUNDED

Technology organises approved sources.

REFLECTION

Only where you expressly permit carefully bounded interpretation.

UNKNOWN

MIA does not have enough evidence to answer honestly.
