Recurring archive model

What the subscriptions are being designed to pay for.

The subscription is not intended to hold memories hostage. It needs to fund a living family archive: secure storage, backup, support, contributors, export, continued story capture and long-term maintenance.

Essential

Planned

Pricing after pilot cost data

Higher storage and included human service credits where sustainable.

These are target commercial bands, not hosted plans currently available for purchase. Final limits and pricing depend on measured storage, backup, support and service costs from the Founding Pilot Programme.
