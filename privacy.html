Privacy

Privacy needs to be as clear as the memories are personal.

The production Privacy Notice is being completed alongside the hosted platform. We will not disguise a placeholder as finished legal information.

Current status: the working MIA application is a local browser prototype. Production hosted accounts, cloud storage and multi-user sharing are not yet live.

What the production notice will cover

  • ORVIA Oversight Ltd's role and contact details
  • Account, payment and support information
  • Uploaded photographs, video, voice, documents and metadata
  • Information about family members and children
  • AI/transcription providers and whether data is retained or used for training
  • Storage location, processors and international transfers
  • Retention, deletion, export and service closure
  • Nominated people, recipients and future-release instructions
  • Your data rights and complaints route

Our current direction

MIA is being designed so original source material is kept separate from transcripts, edited versions and AI-assisted output. Publicity permission for a pilot or customer story is a separate choice from receiving the service.
