Trust before technology

Something this personal deserves clear boundaries.

MIA is being built around consent, original-source preservation, honest product status and human control over sensitive decisions.

Your choices

You decide what is included in a service project. We do not need publicity permission to do the work.

Original before derivative

Original recordings and files stay distinguishable from transcripts, edits and later AI-assisted material.

Nothing invented

If a person never recorded an answer, MIA should not manufacture one and present it as theirs.

Human release decisions

AI will not decide death, capacity, safeguarding, family disputes or whether a recipient is emotionally “ready”.

Portable by design

The working prototype already supports archive export. The production goal is an open archive that remains understandable outside MIA.

Honest product status

Secure hosted accounts, cloud storage, MFA, contributor permissions and verified future-release workflows are not presented as live until they are.

Plain English

What MIA will not promise prematurely.

We will not promise “forever storage”, “guaranteed permanence”, zero staff access, or a production cloud security model until the actual technical and contractual mechanisms exist underneath those statements.

Publicity is separate.

Our Founding Pilot Programme is designed so service participation and public case-study consent are separate choices. A person can receive the full service and keep the entire project private.

Presence stays constrained.

The intended direction is simple: retrieve what somebody actually recorded first. Any future generated representation requires separate governance, consent and clear disclosure.
