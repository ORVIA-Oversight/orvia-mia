FAMILIES APART

SAFE CONNECTION NEEDS MORE THAN TECHNOLOGY.

ORVIA is researching professionally governed technology that may eventually support authorised family communication.

This is not a public contact-recovery sales funnel. There is no consumer purchase button for Bridge or Rebuild.

MIA Bridge

A research concept for carefully governed, authorised communication where families are separated and professionals are already responsible for the contact framework.

MIA Rebuild — Family Time

A later professional-pilot concept exploring how safe, child-led digital interaction could supplement — never replace — accountable professional processes.

Boundaries that must remain explicit.

  • MIA does not determine contact.
  • MIA does not override a court.
  • MIA does not diagnose parental alienation.
  • MIA does not decide whether a parent is safe.
  • Any pilot must be developed with appropriately accountable professional organisations.
