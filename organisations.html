FOR ORGANISATIONS

HELP PEOPLE STAY HUMAN WHEN LIFE OR WORK PULLS THEM APART.

MIA is exploring responsible partnerships where memory, connection and life-story preservation can complement existing human support.

Travelling workforces

Help people working away remain part of everyday family life.

Armed Forces welfare

Explore family memory and connection tools without turning MIA into a military-only product.

Offshore & maritime

Support families during long periods of physical separation.

Veterans & charities

Record life stories, service history and family context with appropriate consent.

Hospice & palliative services

Explore professionally governed Life Story pathways that remain person-led and non-exploitative.

Family support services

Research only where professional governance and safeguarding accountability are clear.

MIA does not claim existing partnerships unless they are formally agreed and verified.
